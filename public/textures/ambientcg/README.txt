PBR texture credits and optimization notes
==========================================

These 1K PBR texture sets are from ambientCG. Color maps were converted to WebP
and non-color maps were losslessly converted to WebP for smaller downloads.

- Wood 095 (Color, NormalGL, Roughness): https://ambientcg.com/a/Wood095
- Concrete 034 (Color, NormalGL, Roughness): https://ambientcg.com/a/Concrete034
- Wood Floor 051 (Color, NormalGL, Roughness): https://ambientcg.com/a/WoodFloor051
