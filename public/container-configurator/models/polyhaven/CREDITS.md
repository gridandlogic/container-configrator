# Furniture models

These seven models and their included 1K PBR textures are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), under its [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The `scripts/download-polyhaven-furniture.py` script retrieves the original glTF, geometry, and texture files and checks their MD5 hashes.
